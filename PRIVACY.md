# Privacy Policy for Cross Bible

_Last updated: 26 November 2026_

Cross Bible ("the app") is a free, open source Bible reader for Windows, Linux, macOS, Android and iOS. This policy explains what happens with your data when
you use it. The short version: **the app does not collect, track or sell any personal data.**

## What we collect

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. We do not receive any information about you
or how you use the app.

## What is stored on your device

The following stays on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else:

- Your settings (theme, fonts, font size, language, interface scaling and similar preferences).
- The Bibles and dictionaries you downloaded or imported.
- Your reading position, history and any text you edit or add inside the app.

Uninstalling the app, or deleting its data folder, removes all of this.

## Network connections

The app connects to the internet only when you open its Bible library and download a Bible. These requests go directly from your device to the following
third-party hosts:

- ebible.org
- crosswire.org
- biblia-del-pueblo.github.io (Biblia Libre)

These servers will see the technical information that any web request involves, such as your IP address and the time of the request, and they handle it under
their own privacy policies. The app does not send them any personal information, and we do not operate these servers or receive their logs. If you use the app
offline, with the Bibles already on your device, it makes no network connections at all. On Android the app requests the `INTERNET` permission for this
purpose only.

## Files you import

If you import a Bible file or archive, it is read locally on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere.

## Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

## Third parties and the app stores

If you install the app through the Microsoft Store, Google Play, the App Store or another package source, that store may collect its own data (for example
installation counts or crash reports) under its own policy. We have no control over this and receive no personal data from it.

## Open source

The full source code is public at <https://github.com/simonegli8/CrossBible>, so you can verify the statements above yourself.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this policy will be updated before the change is released, and the date above will change.
